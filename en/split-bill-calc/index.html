FAQ

Is this bill split calculator really free?

Yes, no sign-up or installation required, and there's no limit on how many times you can use it.

What happens if the total doesn't divide evenly?

Any leftover cents are automatically assigned to the first few people, one cent at a time. For example, splitting $100 three ways gives $33.34, $33.33, $33.33.

How is settle-up mode different?

Split evenly assumes everyone pays the same amount. Settle-up mode is for when everyone actually paid a different amount (e.g. one person paid the full bill), comparing against the average to work out who should send what to whom, using the fewest transfers possible.

Is the amount I enter sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser, the amounts and names you enter are never sent to a server.
