FAQ

Is this BMI calculator really free?

Yes, no sign-up or installation required, and there's no limit on how many times you can use it.

Is my height, weight, and age sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser, nothing you enter is uploaded or stored anywhere.

Does a normal BMI mean I am healthy?

BMI is a reference figure calculated only from height and weight, it does not account for muscle mass or body fat percentage. For example, someone with a lot of muscle may have a high BMI but low body fat. Always consult a doctor or professional for an accurate health assessment.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the minimum energy your body uses at complete rest. TDEE adds your daily activity level on top of that to estimate the total calories you burn in a day. TDEE is usually the reference figure used for diet or meal planning.
