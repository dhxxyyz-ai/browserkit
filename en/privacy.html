Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026  |  Last Updated: June 11, 2026

Every BrowserKit service uses fully local, serverless processing.
Files you upload are processed entirely within your browser
and are never sent to or stored on an external server.

1. Information We Collect

BrowserKit does not require sign-up or any personal information to use the service.

However, the following information may be collected automatically to operate Google AdSense advertising.

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Cookies and advertising identifiers (provided by Google AdSense)

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • Operating the service and improving quality
  • Displaying ads and measuring ad performance (Google AdSense)
  • Analyzing usage statistics

3. How Uploaded Files Are Processed

Files you upload (images, PDFs, etc.) are processed entirely within your browser (client-side).
File data is never sent to an external server, and is automatically discarded when your session ends.
  • Files are never stored on a server.
  • OCR processing for the image-masking tools runs entirely in your browser via Tesseract.js.
  • Processing results are saved only to your own device.
  • Exception — CurrencyCalc: To fetch current exchange rates, this tool communicates with an external exchange-rate API (ExchangeRate-API). Only currency codes are transmitted — the amounts you enter or your calculation results are never sent.
  • Exception — BGRemove: On first use, this tool downloads the AI model file used for background removal from a CDN. Your image is never sent during this — the actual background-removal processing happens entirely in your browser once the model is ready.
  • Exception — XlsxCsvConvert: This tool downloads an open-source library (SheetJS) used to parse Excel files from a CDN. Your uploaded Excel/CSV file is never sent during this — the actual conversion happens entirely in your browser once the library is ready.

4. Retention Period

BrowserKit does not separately store or retain your personal information. Information collected through Google AdSense is managed in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

BrowserKit does not sell or otherwise disclose collected personal information to outside parties, except in the following cases.

  • Operation of Google AdSense advertising (Google LLC)
  • When required by law or requested by an investigative authority

6. Use of Cookies

BrowserKit may use cookies for ad personalization through Google AdSense.

  • You can decline cookies in your browser settings.
  • Declining cookies may limit some ad features, but will not affect your ability to use the service.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads on Google's Ads Settings page: adssettings.google.com

7. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time.

  • Request to view how your personal information is being processed
  • Withdraw consent to the collection, use, or disclosure of your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information

8. Disclaimer

BrowserKit's automated processing features (masking, conversion, compression, etc.) do not guarantee 100% complete or accurate results. Always review important documents yourself before relying on them. Legal responsibility for the use of any processed output rests with the user.

9. Personal Information Protection Officer

For questions about how your personal information is handled, please contact us at the address below.

  • Email: browserkit777@gmail.com

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised in response to changes in law or in the service. Any changes will be announced on this page.
