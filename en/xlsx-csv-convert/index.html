Frequently asked questions

Is the Excel ↔ CSV Converter really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Is my uploaded Excel file sent to a server?

No. Excel parsing happens entirely in your browser using the open-source SheetJS library, and uploaded files are never sent to an external server.

Can I convert an Excel file with multiple sheets?

Yes. Uploading an Excel file shows a list of its sheets, and you can choose which one to convert to CSV.

Is the older Excel format (.xls) supported?

Yes, you can upload both .xlsx and .xls files. However, converting from CSV to Excel always saves in the newer .xlsx format.
