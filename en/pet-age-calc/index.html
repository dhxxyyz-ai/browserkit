Frequently asked questions

Is the Pet Age Calculator really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no limit on the number of calculations either.

Why do I need to enter a size separately for dogs?

Because larger dogs age faster than smaller ones. According to the AVMA chart, a small dog gains about 4 human years per year, while a giant dog gains about 7.

Does the calculation differ by cat breed too?

No. Unlike dogs, cats don't vary much in size between breeds, so a single conversion table is used.

Does this result reflect my pet's exact health status?

No. The conversion table is a reference figure based on average aging speed; actual aging can vary based on individual health and genetics. Always check with a veterinarian for an accurate assessment of your pet's health.
