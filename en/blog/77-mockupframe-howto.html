Device Mockup Generator: The Complete Guide

July 13, 2026

Uploading an app screenshot to your portfolio as a plain rectangular image looks flat. Showing it as if it's actually on a smartphone or laptop screen looks far more convincing, but compositing a mockup by hand usually means Photoshop layer work. MockupFrame is a free online tool that instantly composites a screenshot into a smartphone, tablet, laptop, or browser-window mockup frame. Adjust the background color and padding and download it as a PNG right away, and everything happens entirely in your browser.

Key Features

📱
4 Device Frames
Choose the mockup you want -- smartphone, tablet, laptop, or browser window.
🎨
Background Presets
Offers solid-color and gradient background presets so you can change the mood with a single click.
📐
Adjustable Padding
Adjust the padding around the mockup with a slider to get the composition you want.
🔒
Fully Local Processing
Your uploaded screenshot is composited entirely in your browser and never sent to a server.

How to Use It

1

Upload a Screenshot

Click the upload area or drag a screenshot onto it. The image fills the screen area edge-to-edge (a "cover" fit), so part of it may be cropped if the aspect ratio differs.

2

Choose a Device and Background

Pick the mockup you want -- smartphone, tablet, laptop, or browser -- from the tabs at the top, then adjust the overall mood with the background presets and padding slider.

3

Check the Result and Download

The finished mockup appears immediately in the preview. If you like it, save it with the ⬇ Download PNG button, or click ↺ Choose New Image to try a different screenshot.

⚠️ Note: These are mockup frames drawn with simple shapes, not photos of actual devices. If you need a highly detailed photorealistic device image, we recommend also using a dedicated mockup tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Device Mockup Generator tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. What device frames are supported?

You can composite a screenshot into a variety of device frames, including smartphones, to create a mockup image that looks like it's on an actual device.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
