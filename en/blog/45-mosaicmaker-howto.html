Mosaic Maker: The Complete Guide

July 9, 2026

Sometimes you want to hide something in a screenshot -- a price tag, a game-ending spoiler -- that isn't a face or personal info, but you still want it blocked out. MosaicMaker is a free online tool for freely dragging over any area of an image to apply a mosaic. There's no automatic detection step -- you can start selecting and editing the moment you upload, and everything happens entirely in your browser, so your image is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🖱️
Free-Drag Area Selection
Drag over any area on the canvas and a mosaic is applied to it instantly. No waiting for a detection result.
🧱
Adjustable Mosaic Intensity
Use the block-size slider (6px-40px) to adjust the chunkiness of the mosaic.
📍
Multiple Areas + Individual Delete
Hide multiple spots at once, and delete just the ones you want from the area list.
↩️
Undo (Ctrl+Z)
If you made a mistake with an area you just selected, undo it instantly with the Undo button or Ctrl+Z.

How to Use It

1

Upload an Image

Click the upload zone or drag an image onto it. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported. There's no separate detection process -- the editing screen appears the moment you upload.

2

Drag Over the Area to Hide

On desktop, click and drag with your mouse to select an area. On mobile, tap the starting point once, then tap the ending point again to confirm the area (a two-tap method). Repeat this as many times as you need to hide multiple spots.

3

Adjust Mosaic Intensity

Moving the block-size slider changes the mosaic particle size for every area already selected, all at once. The larger the value, the harder it is to make out the original shape.

4

Delete or Undo an Area

Delete a mistakenly selected area individually with the ✕ button in the list, or undo areas in order starting from the most recent with ↩ Undo (or Ctrl+Z).

5

Download

Click the ⬇ Download PNG button to save the finished image.

How Is This Different from MaskPrivacy, MaskFace, and MaskDoc?

BrowserKit already has tools that automatically find and mask personal information and faces. MosaicMaker serves a fundamentally different purpose.

MaskPrivacy / MaskPDF / MaskFaceMosaicMaker
Motivation"I need to hide my personal info or face""I want to hide this part" (whatever the reason)
FlowAuto-detect → review results → manually adjust if neededNo detection step -- freely drag to edit right after upload
First screenA list of detection resultsThe editing canvas, immediately
What it hidesText (like ID numbers), facesAnywhere in the image (logos, objects, backgrounds, text -- anything)
ToneLegal / securityEditing / fun

The other three tools put the detection engine front and center; MosaicMaker puts fast editing UX front and center. If you want to reliably find and hide personal information, use MaskPrivacy, MaskPDF, or MaskFace. If you just want to quickly hide any part of an image regardless of why, use MosaicMaker.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Mosaic Maker tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Can I mosaic just a specific part of an image?

Yes, you can select any area you want and apply a mosaic to just that part.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
