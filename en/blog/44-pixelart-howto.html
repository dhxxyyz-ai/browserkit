Convert to Pixel Art: The Complete Guide

July 9, 2026

Want to give a profile photo that 8-bit game look, but drawing pixel art by hand feels out of reach? PixelArt is a free online tool that instantly converts an image into a pixel-art style by averaging blocks of pixels, just like retro dot-graphic game art. Control the level of detail with block size, and turn on grid lines to make each pixel stand out. Everything happens entirely in your browser, so your uploaded image is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🧊
Adjustable Block Size
Adjust block size from 4px to 32px. The larger the value, the chunkier the dots and the simpler the original shape becomes.
🎮
Quick Presets
Apply with one click using 4 presets: Fine (6px) / Default (12px) / Chunky (24px) / Retro Dots (32px).
🔺
Grid Lines
Turn on the checkbox to show faint grid lines between blocks, making each pixel unit stand out more clearly.
🖼️
Live Preview
Adjusting the slider or a preset is reflected on the result canvas on the right instantly.

How to Use It

1

Upload an Image

Click the upload zone or drag an image onto it. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported. Once uploaded, the converted result appears instantly at the default value (12px blocks).

2

Adjust Block Size

Click a preset button or drag the slider directly to adjust block size. The larger the blocks, the bigger each dot in the image and the simpler the shape becomes.

Quick Presets

🔎 Fine (6px) 🎮 Default (12px) 🧱 Chunky (24px) 👾 Retro Dots (32px)
3

Show Grid Lines (Optional)

Turn on the Show Grid Lines checkbox to draw faint lines between blocks so each pixel appears separated. Useful when you want to emphasize a retro game sprite look.

4

Download

Click the ⬇ Save PNG button to download the finished pixel art image.

👾

📱

Apply a pixel-art filter to an ordinary photo to make a distinctive profile picture.
🎨

🔒 PixelArt processes everything entirely in your browser. Since uploaded images are never sent to an external server, you can safely convert photos you haven't published yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Convert to Pixel Art tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Can I adjust the pixel size?

Yes, you can adjust the pixel size to change the chunkiness and feel of the dots however you like.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
