GIF Animation Maker: The Complete Guide

July 18, 2026

Want to show a series of screenshots explaining how to use something, but uploading them one at a time is a hassle and a video feels like overkill? GifMaker is a free online tool that composites multiple uploaded images into an animated GIF once you set their order and frame timing. It's handy for turning a burst-shot photo sequence into a short animated loop, or stitching together a series of screenshots, and you can choose whether it loops and what size to output. Everything happens entirely in your browser.

Key Features

🖼️
Multi-Image Upload and Reordering
Upload multiple images at once, and freely reorder frames by dragging.
⏱️
Adjustable Frame Timing
Fine-tune the frame transition speed with a slider from 50ms to 2000ms.
🔁
Choose Looping
Choose a GIF that loops infinitely, or one that plays just once.
🔒
Fully Local Processing
Your uploaded images and the resulting GIF are processed entirely in your browser and never sent to a server.

How to Use It

1

Upload Images

Click the upload area or drag multiple images onto it to turn into a GIF. You can reorder frames by dragging in the frame list, or delete a frame you don't need.

2

Set Frame Timing, Output Size, and Looping

Set the transition speed with the Frame Timing slider, the GIF size with the Output Size (320/480/640px) buttons, and whether it loops with the Loop checkbox.

3

Generate and Download the GIF

Click the 🎞️ Generate GIF button to see a progress indicator, and once it finishes, a result preview and a ⬇ Download GIF button appear.

⚠️ Note: The more images and the larger the output size, the longer GIF generation takes. For smooth processing, we recommend 10 images or fewer and an output size of 640px or smaller. Every image is cropped to fit (cover) the first image's aspect ratio.

Options at a Glance

OptionDescription
Frame timingAdjustable from 50ms (fast) to 2000ms (slow) -- shorter means faster transitions
Output sizeChoose 320 / 480 / 640px -- larger means better quality but a bigger file and longer generation time
LoopOn loops infinitely; off plays once and stops on the last frame

🔒 GifMaker runs all processing entirely in your PC's browser. Your uploaded images and the resulting GIF are never sent to an external server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the GIF Animation Maker tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded images sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded images are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. How many images can I add?

There is no hard limit, but the more images and the larger the output size, the longer GIF generation takes. For smooth processing, we recommend 10 images or fewer and an output size of 640px or smaller.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
