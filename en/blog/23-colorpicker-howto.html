Pick Color: The Complete Guide

June 19, 2026

Ever wondered exactly what color that background in a design you liked actually is? Eyeballing a close match usually ends up subtly off. ColorPicker is a free online tool that picks the exact color at the pixel you click on an image. Hover over it and an 8x magnifier appears so you can zero in precisely, and you can instantly copy the picked color as HEX, RGB, or HSL. Everything happens entirely in your browser, so your image is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🔍
8x Magnifier
Shows the area around your cursor magnified 8x, so you can pick the exact pixel you want.
📋
Copy 3 Formats
Copy HEX, RGB, or HSL individually to your clipboard, ready to paste straight into your dev or design tool.
🎨
Save to Palette
Every click automatically saves the color to your palette. Duplicates are removed automatically, and you can export the whole palette as text.
✨
Auto-Extract Dominant Colors
One click analyzes the image and extracts up to 10 dominant colors automatically.

Basic Usage

1

Upload an Image

Click the upload area or drag an image onto it. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported.

2

Hover to Preview a Color

Hover over the image and the 8x magnifier appears. As you move around, the current color updates live in the panel on the right.

3

Click to Save the Color

Click on the color you want. It's automatically saved to the palette on the right, with a toast message confirming it.

4

Copy the Code

Click the Copy button next to whichever format you want (HEX / RGB / HSL) in the right panel. It's copied straight to your clipboard.

Color Code Format Guide

ColorPicker provides the same color in three formats at once. Pick whichever matches the tool you're using.

HEX
#6C63FF
RGB
rgb(108, 99, 255)
HSL
hsl(243, 100%, 70%)

Using the Palette

Saving and Reselecting Colors

Every click on the image automatically adds that color to your palette. The same color won't be saved twice. Clicking a palette swatch brings that color back up in the right panel so you can copy its code again.

Exporting the Palette

Click Export and every saved color is copied to your clipboard in the format below, ready to paste into Notion, a text editor, or a spreadsheet.

Export Format (Example)
#6C63FF   |   rgb(108,99,255)   |   hsl(243, 100%, 70%)
#34D399   |   rgb(52,211,153)   |   hsl(158, 62%, 52%)
#F87171   |   rgb(248,113,113)   |   hsl(0, 91%, 71%)

Auto-Extracting Dominant Colors

No need to click through the whole image one color at a time. Click ✨ Auto-Extract Dominant Colors and it analyzes the image in a 5×5 grid, averages each region's color, merges similar ones, and shows up to 10 dominant colors at once.

Click an extracted swatch to save it to your palette and see its code. Great for analyzing brand colors, identifying a photo's dominant colors, or organizing design references.

Use Cases

Web Development — Extracting Colors from a Mockup

Upload a mockup from your designer and click through the key colors. Copy the HEX code and paste it straight into your CSS — no more guessing at color values by hand.

Building a Brand Guide

Upload your company logo and run auto-extract. Save the extracted colors to your palette, then export them into a list of color codes.

Building a Palette from a Photo

Upload a photo you like and click to save 5-6 key colors. Exporting the palette gives you a color set that captures the mood of the photo — great for interior design, fashion, or planning a social feed's color scheme.

💡 ColorPicker processes everything entirely in your browser. Since uploaded images are never sent to an external server, you can safely analyze copyrighted images or internal design mockups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Pick Color tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. What format are the extracted color codes provided in?

They are provided in formats you can use directly on the web, like HEX and RGB, and you can copy them with a single click.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
