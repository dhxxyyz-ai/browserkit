Random Picker: The Complete Guide

July 10, 2026

Ever spent forever drawing a random-order ladder game by hand just to decide who takes the penalty at a group dinner? RandomPicker is a free online drawing tool that lets you enter a list of names and spin a wheel to pick a winner, or randomly split everyone into as many teams as you want. Use it fairly for deciding a penalty, a presentation order, or group assignments -- everything happens entirely in your browser, so the names you enter are never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🎡
Spin the Wheel
Shows a list of names as a wheel and spins it to randomly pick one winner.
👥
Split into Teams
Set the number of teams you want, and everyone is randomly split as evenly as possible.
🏆
Winner History
Turn on "Exclude winners from the next draw" to pick several people in order, with history recorded automatically.
🔒
Fully Local Processing
Every draw runs in your browser. The names you enter are never sent to a server.

Tab Layout

🎡 Spin the Wheel
👥 Split into Teams

The Spin the Wheel tab picks one winner, and the Split into Teams tab divides everyone into multiple teams.

How to Spin the Wheel

1

Enter Participant Names

Enter one participant name per line in the input box. Use the 📋 Fill Sample Names button to fill in sample names right away. Names appear on the wheel the instant you type them.

2

Start the Draw

Click the 🎯 Start Draw button and the wheel spins for 4 seconds before stopping at a random position. The winner is already fairly decided the instant you click the button -- the spin is just an animation showing the result.

3

Check the Winner

Once it stops, the name the pointer lands on is shown prominently on the winner card. To draw multiple times, turn on "Exclude winners from the next draw" to rank people with no repeats.

How to Split into Teams

1

Enter Names and Set the Number of Teams

Enter participant names in the Split into Teams tab, and set the number of teams using the +/− buttons or by typing a number.

2

Run the Split

Click the 🔀 Split into Teams button to shuffle everyone randomly and distribute them as evenly as possible. If the count doesn't divide evenly by the number of teams, some teams may have one extra person.

3

Copy the Result

Use the 📋 Copy Result button at the bottom to copy the current tab's result (winner history or team split) to your clipboard as text.

How Fairness Is Ensured

FeatureMethodHow Fairness Is Ensured
Spin the WheelThe winning index is decided first with Math.random()The winner is already set before the spin animation starts, so the presentation and result are decoupled
Split into TeamsThe Fisher-Yates shuffle algorithmEvery participant has an equal chance of landing in any position

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Random Picker tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Is what I enter sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and what you enter is never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Is the drawing result fair?

The drawing is random and number-based, so it never favors any particular entry.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
