Unit Converter: The Complete Guide

June 20, 2026

Do you keep typing questions into a search bar like "how many kg is 5 lbs" or "how many cm is a 60-inch fabric width"? UnitConvert is a free online unit converter for length, weight, temperature, area, and speed, all in real time. Enter a value and every supported unit's converted result shows at once, and you can copy any of them with a single click. Use it right in your browser with no install or sign-up.

Key Features

⚡
Live Conversion
Every supported unit's result appears in the table the instant you type a value. No separate convert button needed.
📊
Full Unit Table
See every unit in the selected category on one screen. The currently selected unit is highlighted in purple.
📋
Copy Results
Copy a value and its unit symbol straight to your clipboard with the copy button next to each result.
🗂️
5 Categories
Choose the Length, Weight, Temperature, Area, or Speed tab to convert by category. 32 units are supported in total.

Basic Usage

1

Choose a Category Tab

Choose the category you want to convert from the tabs at the top -- Length, Weight, Temperature, Area, or Speed.

2

Choose the Base Unit

Choose the unit of your input value from the dropdown. For example, to convert 100km to other units, choose "Kilometer (km)" from the dropdown.

3

Enter a Value

Enter the number you want to convert into the input box. Every unit's result appears in the table below the instant you type.

4

Copy a Result (Optional)

Click the Copy button on the row for the unit you want, and the value plus its unit symbol are copied together to your clipboard.

🌡️ Temperature (3 Units)

Temperature is converted precisely using formulas, not a simple ratio.

UnitSymbolFormula (from Celsius)
Celsius°CBase unit
Fahrenheit°F°F = °C × 9/5 + 32
KelvinKK = °C + 273.15
🌡️ Example: 100°C = 212°F = 373.15K. 0°C is 32°F and 273.15K. Absolute zero is -273.15°C = 0K.
