Adjust Image: The Complete Guide

June 22, 2026

Took a photo in a dim room and just want to nudge the brightness up before printing it, but installing a heavy editing app feels like overkill? ImageBright is a free online tool that adjusts brightness, contrast, saturation, color temperature, and sharpness with real-time sliders. You can adjust it right in your browser with no app install, and get the look you want instantly with 6 quick presets. Everything happens entirely in your browser, so your image is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🎨
5 Adjustment Sliders
Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, color temperature, and sharpness with sliders. The result updates the instant you change a value.
⚡
6 Quick Presets
Click Vivid, Warm, Cool, Bright, Dark, or B&W and the sliders are set automatically.
📷
Before / After Comparison
Shows the original and adjusted result side by side. A checkerboard background makes transparent pixels easy to spot.
⬇
Save as JPG / PNG
Download the adjusted image as JPG (high quality, 0.92) or PNG (lossless).

Basic Usage

1

Upload an Image

Click the upload zone or drag an image onto it. JPG, PNG, WEBP, and GIF are supported.

2

Adjust Sliders or Pick a Preset

Drag a slider left or right, or click a quick preset button. The result panel on the right updates instantly.

3

Save

Download the adjusted image with the Save as JPG or Save as PNG button.

SliderRangeEffect
☀️ Brightness -100 to +100 Raises or lowers the R, G, B values of every pixel by a fixed amount. Positive brightens, negative darkens.
◐ Contrast -100 to +100 Makes bright areas brighter and dark areas darker, sharpening the tonal difference. Negative values reduce contrast for a hazier look.
🌈 Saturation -100 to +100 Controls color intensity. Positive deepens colors, negative fades them, and -100 turns the image black and white.
🌡️ Temperature -100 to +100 Positive shifts the tone toward red (warmer), negative toward blue (cooler). Useful for correcting indoor lighting.
📷 Sharpness 0 to 100 Uses an unsharp mask algorithm to sharpen edges. Too much can introduce noise.

How It Works

ImageBright reads the entire pixel array with the Canvas API's getImageData() and computes each pixel's R, G, B values directly. It is built with pure JavaScript, with no external image-editing library.

AdjustmentAlgorithm
BrightnessAdds or subtracts a fixed value from every pixel's R, G, B
ContrastMultiplies by a factor of (259×(c+255))/(255×(259-c)) around the midpoint (128)
SaturationConverts RGB → HSL → adjusts the S (saturation) value → converts back to RGB
TemperatureRaises the R channel (warmer) and lowers the B channel (cooler), or the reverse
SharpnessEmphasizes edge pixels with a 4-neighbor unsharp mask convolution

Things to Know

ItemDetails
Excessive sharpnessSetting sharpness too high can introduce noise or over-emphasize edges. A range of 20-40 is recommended.
Large imagesBecause processing works pixel by pixel, very large images can take longer. They are automatically resampled to 2000px or smaller.
GIF animationOnly the first frame of an uploaded GIF is processed. The animation is not preserved.
JPG lossSaving as JPG involves some compression loss. Save as PNG if you need to preserve full quality.
✨ ImageBright processes everything entirely in your browser. Since uploaded images are never sent to an external server, you can safely adjust personal photos or sensitive images.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Adjust Image tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Does adjusting the image damage the original?

No. The original file is left untouched -- the adjusted result is downloaded as a new file, so the original is never modified.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
