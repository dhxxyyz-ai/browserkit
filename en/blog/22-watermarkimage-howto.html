Add Watermark: The Complete Guide

June 19, 2026

Post a portfolio or product photo online, and it can end up copied without credit. A single watermark cuts unauthorized use way down, but firing up Photoshop every time gets old. WatermarkImage is a free online tool that freely composites a text or image watermark onto your photos. There's nothing to install — it runs right in your browser, and since everything happens only there, your original image is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🔤
Text Watermark
Type any text and freely adjust its size, color, and opacity. Choose from 5 color presets or pick a custom color.
🖼️
Image Watermark
Upload a separate logo or signature image to composite onto the photo. Supports transparent PNG backgrounds, ideal for logos.
📍
9-Way Position Picker
Set the watermark position intuitively with 9 buttons spanning top/middle/bottom × left/center/right.
📋
Tiled Repeat
Repeat the watermark across the entire image — effective for deterring unauthorized use.

Basic Usage

1

Upload the Original Image

Click the upload area or drag an image onto it to load your original photo. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported.

2

Choose the Watermark Type

Choose the 🔤 Text Watermark or 🖼️ Image Watermark tab.

3

Set the Options

Adjust the text (or watermark image), size, color, opacity, and position however you like.

4

Preview and Download

Click ✨ Preview Watermark to check the result, then ⬇ Download Image to save it. You can choose PNG, JPG, or WEBP as the output format.

Text Watermark Options in Detail

Size Slider

Adjustable from 10px to 120px. Pick a size that fits your image resolution — around 3-5% of the shorter side usually looks natural.

Opacity Slider

Adjustable from 5% to 100%. Too opaque covers the photo, too faint and the watermark disappears — 50-70% is usually a good middle ground.

Image Watermark Tips

Use a Transparent PNG Background

A logo or signature prepared with a transparent PNG background blends naturally into the original image. A white-background image can show up as a visible white box.

Setting the Size

The size slider sets a percentage relative to the original image's width. A range of 5-20% usually looks natural. If the logo covers too much of the photo, check the preview and adjust.

Choosing an Output Format

Use Cases

Copyright Notices on Photos

Type © 2026 Jane Doe into the text watermark, set opacity to 60%, and position it bottom-right for a natural-looking copyright notice on your photo.

Branding Product Photos

Upload a PNG logo as an image watermark, set the size to 15%, and place it bottom-right to quickly brand your product photos.

Deterring Unauthorized Use

Turn on the Tile Repeat checkbox to repeat the watermark across the whole image. Setting the text to semi-transparent deters copying without overwhelming the photo.

💡 WatermarkImage processes everything entirely in your browser. Since your original and watermark images are never sent to an external server, you can safely process even confidential images. No installation or sign-up required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Add Watermark tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Can I adjust the watermark position or opacity?

Yes, for both text and image watermarks you can adjust the position, size, and opacity to composite them exactly how you want.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
