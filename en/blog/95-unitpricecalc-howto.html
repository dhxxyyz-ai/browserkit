Unit Price Calculator: The Complete Guide

July 22, 2026

At the store, which is cheaper: a 500g pack for $3.90, or a 1kg pack for $7.20? It's not obvious at a glance, but converting to a per-100g price makes it clear (500g works out to $0.78 per 100g, while 1kg works out to $0.72 per 100g — the 1kg pack is cheaper). UnitPriceCalc is a free online tool that automatically converts price, size, and unit into a per-g, per-ml, or per-item unit price so you can instantly see which product is the better deal. All calculations happen right in your browser.

Key Features

⚖️
Automatic Unit Price Conversion
Enter the price, size, and unit, and it automatically calculates the price per 100g, per 100ml, or per item.
📋
Compare Multiple Products at Once
Freely add products to compare three, four, or more at the same time.
🏷️
Best-Price Highlight by Category
Products with different measures, like weight, volume, or count, are only compared for best price within their own category.
🔒
Fully Local Processing
Price information you enter is calculated only in your browser and never sent to a server.

How to Use It

1

Enter Price, Size, and Unit for Each Product

For each product you want to compare, enter the price and size, then choose the matching unit (g/kg/ml/L/count).

2

Add More Products if Needed

Use the + Add Product button to add as many products as you want to compare.

3

Check the Results

Click the ⚖️ Compare button and the unit prices are organized by category, with the cheapest product flagged as the best price.

⚠️ Note: Weight (g/kg) and volume (ml/L) are different measures and can't be directly compared. This calculator only compares best price within the same category (weight/volume/count).

How the Unit Price Is Calculated

UnitConversion
g / kgPrice per 100g = price ÷ (size in g) × 100
ml / LPrice per 100ml = price ÷ (size in ml) × 100
countPrice per item = price ÷ quantity
💡 Real example: Say you're choosing between a 500g pack for $3.90 and a 1kg pack for $7.20 → the 500g pack works out to $0.78 per 100g ($3.90÷5), while the 1kg pack works out to $0.72 per 100g ($7.20÷10). The larger 1kg pack is actually cheaper per 100g — a difference you can't see just by looking at the sticker price ($7.20 vs $3.90).

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Unit Price Calculator really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no limit on the number of comparisons either.

Q. Can I compare weight-based and volume-based products together?

Weight (g/kg) and volume (ml/L) are different measures and can't be directly compared. The lowest price is only compared within the same category (weight/volume/count).

Q. Can I compare products sold per item?

Yes, choosing the 'count' unit lets you compare based on the price per item.

Q. Is the price information I enter sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser, and nothing you enter is ever sent to or stored on an external server.
