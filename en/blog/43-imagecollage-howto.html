Image Collage: The Complete Guide

July 9, 2026

Uploading a bunch of trip photos to social media one at a time is tedious, and most collage apps slap on a watermark or push you toward a paid plan. ImageCollage is a free online tool that combines multiple images into a single grid-layout image. Choose from 8 layouts, reorder images by dragging, and fine-tune spacing and fill mode. Everything happens entirely in your browser, so your uploaded images are never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🖼️
Multi-Image Upload
Select multiple images at once or drag and drop them to upload. You can keep adding more afterward with the + Add Image button.
🔲
8 Layouts
Offers 8 grid layouts: 2×2, 3×3, horizontal/vertical splits, a big-photo-plus-small-photos combo, and more.
🖱️
Reorder by Dragging
Drag an item in the image list to change the order it's placed in the collage.
⚙️
Spacing, Background Color, Fill Mode
Adjust the spacing and background color between cells, and choose whether images fill each cell edge-to-edge (crop) or show the entire image (add padding).

How to Use It

1

Upload Images

Click the upload zone or drag multiple images onto it at once. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported.

2

Choose a Layout

Click the grid shape you want in the "Choose Layout" list on the right. It's reflected on the canvas on the left instantly. If you have fewer images than cells, empty cells show a dashed outline; if you have more, cells fill in order starting from the first image.

Layout List

2×2 3×3 2-Way Horizontal 2-Way Vertical 3-Way Horizontal 3-Way Vertical Big Top + 2 Bottom Big Left + 2 Right
3

Reorder and Delete

Drag an item in the image list on the right to change its order. List order determines the order images are placed in the layout cells. Remove an image with its ✕ button.

4

Adjust Spacing, Background Color, and Fill Mode

Use the spacing slider to set the gap between cells, and the background color picker to set the color of the gaps and padding. ✂️ Fill (Crop) crops away the overflow when an image's aspect ratio doesn't match the cell, filling it edge-to-edge; 🖼️ Show Full fits the entire image inside the cell without cropping (leftover space is filled with the background color).

5

Download

Click the ⬇ Download PNG button to save the finished collage.

Fill (Crop) vs. Show Full -- When to Use Which?

If your uploaded images have different aspect ratios, they won't fit a cell perfectly. ImageCollage solves this with two modes.

ModeBehaviorBest For
✂️ Fill (Crop) Crops away whatever overflows so the image fills the cell edge-to-edge. When you want a full, edge-to-edge look, like a social media post
🖼️ Show Full Shrinks the image to fit inside the cell without cropping, showing the whole thing. Leftover space is filled with the background color. When part of the photo (a document, text, etc.) must not be cropped out

Use Cases

✈️
Travel Photo Collections
Combine several photos from a trip into a single collage to share on social media.
🛍️
Showing Products from Multiple Angles
Combine photos of a product from multiple angles into one image to attach to a marketplace or store listing.
👧👦
Comparison / Before-and-After Photos
Use a 2-way horizontal or vertical layout to place before-and-after photos side by side.
📄
Presentation Graphics
Combine multiple screenshots into a single image to insert neatly into a document or presentation slide.
🔒 ImageCollage processes everything entirely in your browser. Since uploaded images are never sent to an external server, you can safely edit photos you haven't published yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Image Collage tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. How many images can I combine?

You can combine as many images as you want into a single collage in whatever arrangement you choose; with a large number of images, processing time will vary depending on your device's performance.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
