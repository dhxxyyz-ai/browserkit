Text Tools: The Complete Guide

June 18, 2026

Writing a cover letter often means opening a separate word counter to hit a "500 character limit," and a list copied from a spreadsheet with duplicate entries mixed in means eyeballing each one to filter them out. TextTools is a free tool that handles counting characters, cleaning whitespace, converting case, and removing duplicates all in one place in your browser. Whatever text you enter is never sent outside your browser -- it's processed entirely inside it.

Key Features (4 Tabs)

📊
Character Count
Shows total characters, characters excluding whitespace, word count, line count, UTF-8 bytes, and sentence count in real time.
🧹
Clean Whitespace / Line Breaks
Supports 6 modes: collapsing repeated spaces/line breaks, trimming, trimming each line, and removing all of either entirely.
🔤
Case Conversion
Converts to 7 formats: UPPER, lower, Capitalize, camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case, and PascalCase.
❌
Remove Duplicates
Removes duplicate lines, empty lines, or both. Also shows how many lines were removed.

Basic Usage

1

Choose a Tab

Pick the feature you want from the tab buttons at the top. Four are available: Character Count, Clean Whitespace/Line Breaks, Case Conversion, and Remove Duplicates.

2

Type or Paste Text

Type or paste text directly into the input area on the left. In the Character Count tab, results update live the instant you type.

3

Choose a Mode and Copy the Result

In the clean/convert/remove tabs, click the mode button you want and the result appears on the right. Use the 📋 Copy Result button to copy it to your clipboard, or ⇄ Result→Input to bring the result back into the input area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Text Tools tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Is what I enter sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and what you enter is never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Can it handle long text without issue?

Yes, since it processes instantly in your browser, a typical document's length converts without any trouble.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
