Photo Metadata Remover: The Complete Guide

July 10, 2026

Photos taken with a phone or camera carry invisible EXIF metadata alongside the image. That can include the device model and the time it was taken, and if location services were on, precise GPS coordinates too. ExifRemover shows you exactly what's hidden in a photo the moment you upload it, and creates a clean new image with everything stripped out in one click. Everything happens entirely in your browser, so your photos are never sent to an external server.

Key Features

📍
GPS Location Lookup
If the photo has location data, it's converted to latitude/longitude and shown, with a link to view it directly on a map.
📷
Camera Info
Lays out the camera manufacturer/model, lens, date taken, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, and focal length in a table.
🧹
One-Click Removal
Download a new image file with all metadata stripped, with a single button click.
🔒
Fully Local Processing
All analysis and removal runs in your browser. Since photos are never sent to a server, you can use it confidently even for sensitive photos.

How to Use It

1

Upload a Photo

Click the upload zone or drag a photo onto it. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported.

2

Review the EXIF Data

As soon as you upload, the photo's EXIF data is automatically analyzed and displayed in a table. If GPS location is present, it's called out with a warning banner at the top, with a "View on Map" link to see the actual location.

Example EXIF Display

3

Remove EXIF and Download

Click ⬇ Remove EXIF and Download and the image, stripped of all metadata, is saved as originalname_no-exif.

⚠️ Note: EXIF removal works by redrawing the image onto a canvas and exporting it. JPEGs may lose a tiny amount of quality from recompression (at 95% quality), but it's essentially imperceptible to the eye. PNGs are processed losslessly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Photo Metadata Remover tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. What information gets removed?

It removes EXIF metadata hidden in the photo, like GPS location, camera device, and the date and time it was taken.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
