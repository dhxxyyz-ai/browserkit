Date Calculator: The Complete Guide

June 23, 2026

Counting days by hand is easy to get wrong -- forget a leap day, and your D-day is off by one. And if you've ever tried to explain the traditional Korean age system, where everyone turns a year older on New Year's Day rather than their birthday, you know it takes a table to keep straight. DateCalc is a free online date calculator that handles D-day calculation, date differences, and age calculation all in one place. It shows international age alongside Korean age, and all calculations happen entirely in your browser.

Key Features

🎯
D-day Calculation
Calculates the days remaining until, or elapsed since, a target date. See it in days, weeks, and months all at once.
📏
Date Difference Calculation
Shows the difference between two dates in detail -- days, weeks, years/months/days, hours, and minutes.
🎂
Age Calculation
Calculates international age, Korean age (traditional counting), and "year age" (calendar-year based) all at once. Also shows total days since birth and days until your next birthday.
📅
Auto-Filled Today's Date
Today's date is filled in automatically when you open a tab. Jump back to it anytime with the Set to Today button.

How Each Age System Differs

Korea traditionally uses three different ways of counting age. DateCalc calculates all three and shows them at once.

Age TypeHow It's CalculatedExample (born March 1990, as of June 2026)
International ageCurrent year minus birth year, if the birthday has passed; minus 1 if it hasn't36 (birthday has passed)
Year ageCurrent year minus birth year36
Korean age (traditional counting)Current year minus birth year, plus 137

Since June 2023, international age has been the legal standard for age in Korea. Hospitals, legal documents, and government offices all use international age. In everyday conversation, though, Korean age (traditional counting) is still widely used -- everyone turns a year older together on New Year's Day, rather than on their individual birthday.

💡 A real calculation example: If you started a job on March 1, 2026 and worked until today (July 24, 2026) → that's 145 days between the two dates. Even an odd stretch of time like this converts instantly into a day count in DateCalc -- just enter the start and end dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and what you enter is never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. What kinds of calculations does it support?

It supports D-day calculation, calculating the difference between two dates, and age calculation.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
