Split Bill Calculator: The Complete Guide

July 21, 2026

You've probably been there: dinner with friends, one person pays the whole bill, and now everyone says "let's just split it." But when the total doesn't divide evenly, or someone paid less because they skipped the drinks, the math gets surprisingly fiddly. SplitBillCalc is a free online tool that either splits a total evenly, or, when everyone paid different amounts, automatically figures out who owes whom and how much. All calculations happen right in your browser.

Key Features

👥
Split Evenly
Enter the total and the number of people, and instantly get each person's share.
💸
Settle Up Mode
When everyone paid a different amount, compares each person to the average and works out how to settle up.
➡️
Minimal Transfers
Shows who should pay whom, using the fewest possible transfers.
🔒
Fully Local Processing
Amounts and names you enter are processed only in your browser and never sent to a server.

How to Use It

1

Choose a Mode

If everyone is paying the same amount, choose Split Evenly. If everyone already paid different amounts, choose Settle Up.

2

Enter the Amounts

For Split Evenly, enter the total and the number of people. For Settle Up, enter each person's name (optional) and how much they paid, and use + Add Person to add more.

3

Calculate

Click the 🧮 Calculate button and see each person's share, or the settle-up result. In Settle Up mode, it also spells out exactly who should pay whom.

⚠️ Note: In Split Evenly, any leftover cents that don't divide evenly are distributed by having the first person(s) in line pay one cent more. Feel free to round however's convenient when actually settling up.
💡 Real example: Three friends have dinner, total $60, and Alex pays for everyone → the average share is $20 per person. Blake and Casey each send Alex $20 and they're settled. If instead $10 is split three ways evenly, it comes to $3.34 / $3.33 / $3.33 — the extra cent goes to whoever's first in line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Split Bill Calculator really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no limit on the number of calculations either.

Q. What happens if the total doesn't divide evenly among everyone?

Any leftover cents are automatically distributed by having the first person(s) in line pay one cent more. For example, splitting $10 three ways shows as $3.34 / $3.33 / $3.33.

Q. How is Settle Up mode different?

Split Evenly assumes everyone pays the same amount, while Settle Up handles the case where everyone paid a different amount (e.g. one person covered the whole bill), comparing each person to the average and calculating who should pay whom, using the fewest possible transfers.

Q. Is the amount I enter sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser, and no amount or name you enter is ever sent to an external server.
