Focus & Break Timer: The Complete Guide

July 10, 2026

If you're reaching for your phone within 5 minutes of starting to study or work, the problem might not be willpower -- it might be structure. The Pomodoro Technique forcibly interrupts this pattern by alternating 25 minutes of focus with 5 minutes of rest. PomodoroTimer is a free online timer that manages this rhythm for you. Set your focus, short break, and long break durations however you want, and it alerts you with sound and an on-screen notification when a session ends. It tracks accurately by real elapsed time even if you switch tabs, and today's completed session count is recorded automatically in your browser.

Key Features

🍅
Automatic Focus/Break Switching
Automatically switches to break mode when a focus session ends, and back to focus mode when the break ends.
⚙️
Freely Configurable Timing
Change the focus duration, short break, long break, and how many sessions precede a long break however you want.
📊
Today's Completed Sessions
Saves the number of completed focus sessions in your browser so you can see how much you've focused today.
🔔
Sound and Tab Title Alerts
An alert sound plays when a session ends, and you can check the time remaining in the browser tab title even while looking at another tab.

How to Use It

1

Adjust Timing If You Want

The defaults are 25 min focus / 5 min short break / 15 min long break / a long break every 4 sessions. Change these in the ⚙️ Time Settings card below (only while the timer is stopped).

2

Start a Focus Session

Click ▶ Start to begin the countdown, with the circular ring shrinking as time passes. Pause anytime with ⏸ Pause, or restart the current session from the top with ↺ Reset.

3

Check the End-of-Session Alert and Rest

When focus time ends, it automatically switches to break mode with a sound, and today's completed session count goes up by 1. Use ⏭ Skip if you want to jump straight to the next step.

⚠️ Note: Completed-session history is stored only in this browser's localStorage, not on a server, and doesn't sync across devices or browsers. It also resets to 0 once the date changes at midnight. Clearing your browser data erases the history too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Focus & Break Timer tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Is what I enter sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and what you enter is never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. What happens to the timer if I close the tab?

The timer only runs while the browser tab is open, so keep the tab open while using it.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
