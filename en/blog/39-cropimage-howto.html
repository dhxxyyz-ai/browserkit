Crop Image: The Complete Guide

July 4, 2026

Firing up a heavyweight editor just to square off a profile photo or crop a screenshot down to the part you need feels like overkill. CropImage is a free online tool that handles cropping, rotating, and flipping right in your browser. There's nothing to install, and since everything happens only in your browser, your images are never sent to an external server.

Key Features

✂️
Crop
Drag directly on the image to select the area you want. Choose a free-form ratio or a preset.
🪪
ID Photo Ratio
A 3:4 shortcut button quickly sets the ratio commonly used for ID/passport photos.
🔄
Rotate
Rotate 90° clockwise or counterclockwise, instantly switching between landscape and portrait.
↔️
Flip
Flip horizontally (mirror effect) or vertically.
🖥️
Fully Local Processing
Since images are never sent to a server, you can process sensitive photos with confidence.
📥
Instant Download
Download the finished image instantly as a PNG file.

Basic Usage

1

Upload an Image

Drag an image onto the upload area or click to choose a file. JPG, PNG, WEBP, and GIF are supported.

2

Choose a Feature Tab

Choose the ✂️ Crop or 🔄 Rotate/Flip tab. Switching tabs shows that editing area.

3

Apply the Edit

In the Crop tab, pick a ratio, drag on the image to select an area, and click Apply Crop. In the Rotate/Flip tab, clicking a button applies it instantly.

4

Check the Preview

Compare the original and result side by side, checking resolution. Crop and rotate/flip stack on top of each other, so you can switch back and forth between the tabs and edit multiple times.

5

Download the Image

Click Download PNG and the edited image is saved as cropimage_result.png.

✂️ Cropping in Detail

Dragging on the image shows the selected area with a dashed border and corner handles. Pick a ratio button first to lock the aspect ratio while dragging.

Ratio ButtonDescription
FreeSelect any size with no aspect ratio constraint
1:1Square — good for social media profile photos
4:3A common photo ratio
3:4ID/passport photo ratio
16:9Widescreen ratio
9:16Vertical — good for Stories, Shorts, and similar formats

🔄 Rotate/Flip in Detail

Rotate and flip apply instantly on click, and you can press them repeatedly to stack adjustments.

ButtonEffect
Rotate 90° CWRotates the image 90° clockwise (swaps landscape/portrait)
Rotate 90° CCWRotates the image 90° counterclockwise (swaps landscape/portrait)
Flip HorizontalMirrors the image left-to-right
Flip VerticalFlips the image upside down
💡 Crop and rotate/flip both apply to the same working canvas, cumulatively. For example, you can rotate then crop, or crop then rotate again. Download only supports PNG, so use FitImage if you need a different format.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Crop Image tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. What other features are there besides cropping?

It also supports rotating and flipping images horizontally or vertically.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
