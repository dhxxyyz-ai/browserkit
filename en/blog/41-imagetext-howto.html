Add Text to Image: The Complete Guide

July 9, 2026

Want to add a caption to a meme or a card-news graphic, but most watermark tools only let you pin text to one of 9 fixed corner spots? That gets frustrating fast if you want to place it exactly where you want. ImageText is a free online tool for freely placing multiple pieces of text on an image. You can click and drag text directly on the canvas to adjust its position, and everything happens entirely in your browser, so your uploaded image is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🖨️
Multiple Text Layers
Create multiple text layers with the + Add Text button, and select each one from the list to edit it individually.
🖱️
Drag to Reposition
Click and drag text on the canvas to move it freely to wherever you want.
🔠
Font, Size, and Color Settings
Set 4 fonts, size, color, opacity, and bold independently for each text layer.
🖼️
Save as PNG / JPG / WEBP
Download the finished image in whichever format you want. For JPG/WEBP, a transparent background is automatically converted to white.

How to Use It

1

Upload an Image

Click the upload zone or drag an image onto it. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported. On upload, one default text layer is automatically created in the center of the image.

2

Add and Select Text

Click the + Add Text button to create a new text layer. Clicking an item in the text layer list on the left selects that text, and it's shown on the canvas with a purple dashed border.

3

Edit Content and Style

Adjust the text content, font, size, opacity, color, and bold setting in the panel on the right. Changes are reflected on the canvas in real time.

4

Drag to Move

Press down on text with your mouse (or finger) on the canvas and drag it to where you want. If the point you click doesn't land on any text, the drag won't start.

5

Delete Unwanted Text

Click the ✕ button in the text layer list to delete that layer.

6

Choose an Output Format and Download

Choose PNG, JPG, or WEBP, then click the ⬇ Download Image button. The downloaded image will not show the dashed selection border you see while editing.

How Is This Different from WatermarkImage?

BrowserKit has two tools for putting text on an image. Pick whichever fits what you need.

📸
Making Social Card-News Graphics
Place a title, subtitle, source credit, and other text at whatever positions you want over a background image to build a card-news graphic.
🏷️
Labeling Product Photos
Write price, size, contact info, and other details directly onto a marketplace or store product photo.
📅
Making Memes
Add caption-style text above and below a photo to quickly make a fun image.
🗺️
Adding Captions to Travel Photos
Add place names, dates, and other captions at multiple spots on a travel photo to create a keepsake image.
🔒 ImageText processes everything entirely in your browser. Since uploaded images are never sent to an external server, you can safely edit images you haven't published yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Add Text to Image tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Can I change the font or color?

Yes, you can adjust the text's size, color, and position to composite it onto the image in exactly the style you want.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
