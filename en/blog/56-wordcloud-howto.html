Word Cloud Generator: The Complete Guide

July 10, 2026

Reading through dozens of open-ended survey responses one by one just to pull out the key words is a chore for your eyes. WordCloudGen is a free online tool that analyzes word frequency in pasted text and generates a word cloud image, with more frequent words shown larger. Use it to grasp key terms in meeting notes, survey responses, or a blog post at a glance, or to create an eye-catching image to share -- all analysis and image generation happens entirely in your browser, so your text is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

📊
Word Frequency Analysis
Splits text on spaces and punctuation and counts how often each word appears.
🚫
Stop Word Removal
Automatically filters out meaningless connector words like "and" or "but," leaving only the key terms.
🎨
Automatic Layout
Fills the canvas with no gaps using its own spiral-placement algorithm that keeps words from overlapping.
🔒
Fully Local Processing
All analysis and image generation runs inside your browser. Your text is never sent to a server.

How to Use It

1

Enter Text

Paste the text you want to turn into a word cloud into the input box. Use the 📋 Fill Example Text button to try it with sample text right away.

2

Set Options

Adjust the Remove Stop Words checkbox, Max Words (60 by default), and Color Theme (color/monochrome) as you like.

3

Generate the Word Cloud

Click the 🎨 Generate Word Cloud button and an image is created where more frequent words appear larger and less frequent ones smaller. Change the options and click again to regenerate with a new layout.

4

Download as PNG

Save the finished word cloud as an image file with the ⬇ Download PNG button. Use it directly for a blog thumbnail, a presentation, or a social media post.

⚠️ Note: Since words are split on spaces and punctuation, inflected forms in languages with particles or suffixes attached to a root word are counted as separate words without morphological analysis. If you need an exact count, prepare text with those forms normalized, or treat the result as a rough reference.

How Stop Word Removal Works

What Happens
Remove Stop Words on (default)Counts words while excluding those on the built-in stop word list (like "and," "but," "is") and tokens made up only of numbers
Remove Stop Words offCounts every token as-is -- use this when you want to see the frequency of the raw text, unmodified

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Word Cloud Generator tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Is what I enter sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and what you enter is never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Does it support non-English text?

Yes, you can create a word-frequency-based word cloud from non-English text too.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
