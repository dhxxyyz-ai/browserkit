Barcode Generator: The Complete Guide

July 24, 2026

Need to attach a barcode to each item in an inventory spreadsheet, but installing paid barcode software feels like overkill? Or maybe you just need a sample barcode image for a presentation or portfolio, and signing up for a full retail barcode service is more than you need. BarcodeGen is a free online tool that instantly generates a CODE128, EAN13, EAN8, UPC, or CODE39 barcode from text or numbers you enter, downloadable as a PNG. All generation happens entirely in your browser.

How to Use It

1

Enter Text or Numbers, then Choose a Format

Enter the value you want turned into a barcode, and pick the format you want -- CODE128, EAN13, EAN8, UPC, or CODE39.

2

Click Generate Barcode

Click the 📊 Generate Barcode button and a barcode in your chosen format is created instantly.

3

Download as PNG

Save the generated barcode as an image file with the ⬇️ Download as PNG button.

⚠️ Note: EAN13, EAN8, and UPC only accept numbers with a specific digit count per their standard specs (EAN13: 12-13 digits, EAN8: 7-8 digits, UPC: 11-12 digits). Choose CODE128 or CODE39 if you need to use arbitrary characters or symbols.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Barcode Generator really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no limit on how many times you can generate a barcode.

Q. Can I enter any number for EAN13, EAN8, or UPC?

No. EAN13 supports only 12-13 digits, EAN8 only 7-8 digits, and UPC only 11-12 digits. An error message appears if the digit count doesn't match. Choose CODE128 or CODE39 if you need to enter any value freely.

Q. Can I use a generated barcode on a real product?

The barcode image itself is generated correctly to spec, but using it as an actual retail barcode (like a GS1-registered barcode) may require a separate registration process with an issuing authority.

Q. Is the value I enter sent to a server?

No. All generation happens entirely in your browser, and what you enter is never sent to or stored on an external server.
