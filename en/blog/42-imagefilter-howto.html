Image Filters: The Complete Guide

July 9, 2026

You love a certain Instagram filter, but recreating that exact look in another photo editor means fiddling with every value by hand? ImageFilter is a free online tool that applies Instagram-style filters -- black & white, sepia, vintage, and more -- with a single click. After applying a filter you can fine-tune brightness, contrast, and sharpness to nail the exact look you want, and everything happens entirely in your browser, so your uploaded image is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🎨
12 Filters
Apply Black & White, Sepia, Vintage, Blur, Sharpen, Negative, Vignette, Enhanced Contrast, Fog, Sketch, Cartoon, or Night Scene with a single click.
✨
Live Preview
The moment you click a filter, it appears on the result canvas on the right, so you can quickly compare several filters.
⚙️
Fine-Tuning Sliders
After applying a filter, further adjust brightness, contrast, and sharpness to fine-tune the look you want.
🖼️
Save as JPG / PNG
Download the finished image as JPG or PNG.

How to Use It

1

Upload an Image

Click the upload zone or drag an image onto it. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported. Once uploaded, the original appears on the left and the filtered result on the right, side by side.

2

Choose a Filter

Click the filter you want in the filter grid. It's applied to the result canvas on the right instantly, and clicking a different filter automatically deselects the previous one.

Filter List

Original B&W Sepia Vintage Blur Sharpen Negative Vignette Enhanced Contrast Fog Sketch Cartoon Night Scene
3

Fine-Tuning (Optional)

With a filter applied, adjusting the brightness, contrast, and sharpness sliders layers extra correction on top of the filter effect. Slider values persist when you switch filters, so you can lock in brightness and contrast you like first, then compare filters against them.

4

Save

Click the ⬇ Save JPG or ⬇ Save PNG button to download the finished image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Image Filters tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. What filters are available?

You can compare a variety of preset filters in a live preview and choose the mood you want.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
