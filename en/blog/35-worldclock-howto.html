World Clock: The Complete Guide

June 23, 2026

Trying to schedule a video call across time zones and having to search "what time is 2pm my time over there?" every single time? Throw daylight saving into the mix and it gets even more confusing. WorldClock is a free online world clock that shows city times worldwide live and converts between time zones. Use it to coordinate a meeting with an overseas team, plan a travel itinerary, or check a partner's business hours -- it runs entirely on the browser's built-in Intl API with no external API, so your data is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🕑
Live World Clock
The time for your selected cities updates automatically every second. Your location is detected and shown automatically.
🔍
Search and Add Cities
Search for a city by name and add it to your clock list. Cities you add are kept even after a refresh.
🔄
Time Zone Converter
Pick a source and destination city and enter a time, and the equivalent time in the destination city is calculated instantly. The source city is automatically set to your location.
💾
Auto-Saved Settings
Your added city list is saved automatically in your browser. The cities you added are still there the next time you visit.

Using the Time Zone Converter Tab

Use this to convert a specific time into another time zone. For example, you can instantly calculate "what time is 3pm in New York over in Tokyo?"

1

Choose a Source City

Select the city you want to convert from. Your location (time zone) is selected automatically when you open the page -- for example, it defaults to New York if you're browsing from the US, or the appropriate local city elsewhere.

2

Choose a Destination City

Select the city whose local time you want to see. The default is Tokyo. Choose from over 100 cities, listed in order of UTC offset.

3

Enter the Time to Convert

Enter a date and time. The current date and time are filled in by default.

4

Check the Result

The equivalent time in the destination city appears the moment you enter a value. The date and day of week are shown too, so you can immediately see if the date rolls over.

How It Works

WorldClock is built on the browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat API. It runs entirely locally with no external time API or server communication.

FeatureTechnology UsedNotes
Live time displayIntl.DateTimeFormat + setIntervalAuto-updates every second, no server needed
Auto-detecting your locationIntl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions()Your browser detects your time zone automatically
Time zone conversionDate object + timeZone optionAutomatically accounts for daylight saving time (DST)
Saving your city listlocalStoragePreserved across refreshes and return visits
⏰ WorldClock automatically accounts for daylight saving time (DST). The UTC offset for regions that observe DST, like the US and Europe, adjusts automatically by season. New York, for example, shows UTC -5:00 in winter and UTC -4:00 when DST is in effect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the World Clock tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Is what I enter sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and what you enter is never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. Is the displayed time accurate?

It converts each city's time zone based on your device's current time, so it's accurate as long as your device clock is accurate.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
