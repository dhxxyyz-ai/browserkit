Face Blur: The Complete Guide

June 17, 2026 (updated July 3, 2026)

Before posting a group photo, do you find yourself manually editing out the faces of people who didn't consent to being shown? With a lot of people in frame, that takes a while. MaskFace is a free online tool that uses AI to automatically detect faces in an image and mask them with a mosaic or a black box. Face detection runs entirely in your browser, so the original image is never sent to an external server.

Key Features

🤖
Automatic Face Detection
An AI model automatically detects faces in the image, handling multiple people at once.
🔲
Mosaic Blur
Pixelates detected face areas so they're unrecognizable.
⬛
Black Box
Completely covers face areas with a solid black box.
🎚️
Adjustable Mosaic Strength
Fine-tune the mosaic block size from 4px to 32px with a slider.
✍️
Manual Drag Masking
Drag directly over the result image to mask any face that's hard to auto-detect.
📥
Download PNG
Download the finished image instantly as maskface_result.png.

Basic Usage

1

Wait for the Model to Load

The AI model loads automatically when you open the page. This takes about 5-10 seconds, and uploading is enabled once the Face detection model ready message appears.

2

Upload an Image

Drag an image onto the upload area or click to choose a file. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported.

3

Automatic Face Detection

Once the image is uploaded, the AI model automatically detects faces and shows the count of detected faces in the result area.

4

Choose Masking Options

Choose Mosaic or Black Box, and adjust the block size with the mosaic strength slider. The result image updates live as you change the options.

5

Manual Touch-Up and Download

If automatic detection missed a face, drag over it on the result image to mask it manually. When you're done, click Download PNG to save it as maskface_result.png.

When Automatic Detection Struggles

The AI model is trained mostly on front-facing faces, so detection may fail or be less accurate in the situations below. Use manual drag masking to fill in the gaps.

SituationDescription
Side / three-quarter profileSince the model is trained mostly on front-facing faces, profile detection is limited.
Wearing a maskDetection rate may drop when a face is partly covered by a mask.
Very small facesFaces that are too small in the image may not be detected.
Strong backlightingDetection can be difficult when facial outlines aren't clear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the Face Blur tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, and uploaded files are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Q. What if a face isn't detected automatically?

You can manually select an area to mask any face automatic detection misses. Faces at a sharp angle may have a lower detection rate.

Q. Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in a mobile browser without needing to install any app.
