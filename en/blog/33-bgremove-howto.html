AI Background Removal: The Complete Guide

June 22, 2026

Want to cleanly strip the background from a profile photo or product shot, but fussing over the lasso tool in Photoshop to catch every stray hair eats up your whole afternoon? BGRemove is a free online tool where AI automatically recognizes and removes an image's background and saves it as a transparent PNG. No install or sign-up needed, and the entire background-removal computation runs right in your PC's browser, so your image is never sent to an external server.

⚠️ Note: Depending on AI recognition accuracy, the background may not be removed perfectly. Complex backgrounds or images with unclear subject edges can leave some areas partially remaining.

Key Features

🤖
Automatic AI Background Removal
An ONNX-based AI model tells your subject apart from the background and removes it automatically. Just upload an image -- no settings needed.
🔒
Fully Local Processing
AI inference runs directly in your PC's browser. Since images are never sent to an external server, you can safely process personal photos too.
🖼️
Save as Transparent PNG
Saves the background-removed result as a PNG with a transparent background, ready to composite onto other images or use directly in design work.
⚡
Uses Browser Caching
Downloads a roughly 40MB model on your first visit, but loads it instantly from the browser cache after that. Wait times drop significantly on return visits.

Basic Usage

1

Upload an Image

Click the upload zone or drag an image onto it. JPG, PNG, and WEBP are supported.

2

AI Model Loading and Processing (Automatic)

Starts automatically once you upload an image. On a first visit, it downloads the AI model (about 40MB) and then runs background removal. On a return visit, it loads instantly from cache.

3

Check the Result

Compares the original and background-removed result side by side. The checkerboard pattern indicates transparent areas.

4

Save the PNG

Click the Save PNG (Transparent Background) button to download the result. The file is saved as originalfilename_bgremoved.png.

How the AI Model Works

BGRemove uses the open-source @imgly/background-removal library. This library runs an ONNX (Open Neural Network Exchange) segmentation model directly in the browser via WebAssembly (WASM).

ComponentRole
ONNX model (small, ~40MB)The AI segmentation model that tells the subject apart from the background in an image
ONNX Runtime Web (WebAssembly)The inference engine that runs the AI model in the browser
Browser cacheStores the model file after the first visit to avoid re-downloading
Canvas APIApplies the mask to make background pixels transparent
🔒 BGRemove runs all AI inference entirely in your PC's browser. Since images are never sent to an external server, you can safely process sensitive images too -- personal photos, company product images, and more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the AI Background Removal tool really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no usage limit either.

Q. Are uploaded files sent to a server?

The image itself is never sent externally. Only the AI model file is downloaded on first run, and the background removal itself happens entirely in your browser.

Q. Why does the first run take longer?

Because the AI model file is downloaded on the first run. Once the model is ready, the image processing itself happens entirely in your browser.
