FAQ

Is this unit price calculator really free?

Yes — no sign-up or installation required, and there's no limit on how many times you can use it.

Can I compare weight-based and volume-based products together?

No. Weight (g/kg) and volume (ml/L) use different bases and can't be compared directly. The tool only compares the lowest price within the same category (weight / volume / count).

Can I compare products sold per item?

Yes — select "each" as the unit and it will compare based on price per item.

Is my pricing data sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser — nothing you enter is uploaded or stored anywhere.
