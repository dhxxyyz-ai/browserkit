Frequently asked questions

What are the WCAG AA/AAA thresholds?

Under WCAG 2.1, normal text must reach at least 4.5:1 for AA and 7:1 for AAA. Large text (18pt or larger, or 14pt bold) must reach at least 3:1 for AA and 4.5:1 for AAA.

Is the Color Contrast Checker really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no limit on how many times you can check.

Is the color information I enter sent to a server?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser, and the colors you enter are never sent to or stored on an external server.

Why does accessibility contrast matter?

Low-contrast text is hard to read not only for people with low vision or color blindness, but also for anyone viewing a screen outdoors. Government and corporate websites are often required to meet accessibility guidelines.
