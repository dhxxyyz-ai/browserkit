Frequently asked questions

Is the Barcode Generator really free?

Yes, it is completely free to use with no sign-up or installation required. There is no limit on how many you can generate.

Can I enter any numbers for EAN13, EAN8, or UPC?

No. EAN13 supports 12-13 digits, EAN8 supports 7-8 digits, and UPC supports 11-12 digits. An error message appears if the digit count doesn't match. For free-form values, choose CODE128 or CODE39 instead.

Can I use the generated barcode on real products?

The barcode image itself is generated to the correct standard format, but using it as an official retail barcode (such as a GS1-registered barcode) typically requires separate registration with an issuing authority.

Is what I enter sent to a server?

No. All generation happens entirely in your browser, and your input is never sent to or stored on an external server.
