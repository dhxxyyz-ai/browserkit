FAQ

Is this JWT decoder really free?

Yes, no sign-up or installation required, and there's no limit on how many times you can use it.

Is what I enter sent to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser, nothing you enter is uploaded or stored anywhere.

Can I check the token expiration time?

Yes, claims like exp in the payload are shown in a human-readable format.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, it works the same way in your mobile browser with no app install required.
